AWS Architecture Icons
======================

These service icons are the property of Amazon Web Services, Inc.
Sources: https://awsfundamentals.com/aws-icons (the original DynamoDB, S3, Lambda,
CloudWatch, Step Functions and SQS SVGs) and https://aws-icons.com/
(remaining service SVGs served by https://icon.icepanel.io/AWS/svg/).
Related APIs share their parent service icon. ARC entries use the Route 53 icon.
Terms: https://aws.amazon.com/architecture/icons/

They identify emulated AWS services. LocallyCloud is an independent project and
is not affiliated with or endorsed by Amazon Web Services. These assets are not
covered by the Apache-2.0 license of the LocallyCloud source code.

The icons are combined in `sprite.svg` as reusable SVG symbols. Internal IDs
and their references are prefixed per icon to prevent collisions; artwork,
colors, and view boxes are preserved. The dashboard loads symbols with `<use>`.
